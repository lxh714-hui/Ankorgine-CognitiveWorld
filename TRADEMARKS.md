## Code and branding

The selected code license is AGPL-3.0-only. This policy does not add code-license
restrictions, withdraw existing rights or require commercial licensing for
compliant competition. Copyright and trademark rights have different scope.
No trademark registration is asserted.

Proposed brand references include CognitiveWorld, Anchor Cognitive World and
logos whose ownership is verified. Rights holder/contact: **[maintainer to
confirm actual legal identity and contact]**. Mentioning a name does not prove
ownership or exclusivity in all jurisdictions. "Anchor" alone is not claimed
as a registered or universally exclusive mark.

## Accurate references and forks

Reasonable, accurate upstream attribution, independent fork identification,
research citation and compatibility statements are welcome, subject to applicable
law. For example, "an independent fork of CognitiveWorld" or "compatible with
[identified version]" can describe provenance without endorsement. Clearly
identify the independent provider; retain notices required by applicable licenses.

Do not falsely imply that a fork, hosted service, model, package or organization
is official, sponsored, certified, partnered with or operated by maintainers.
Do not misrepresent service origin through names or logos. Distinctive logo or
endorsement permission needs a confirmed rights holder and reviewed process;
this draft grants no rights the maintainer lacks.

## Limits and review

Brand policy protects identity, not architecture ideas, algorithms or compatible
implementations. It does not override lawful nominative/fair use, mandatory
rights or the AGPL, and is not a ban on competitive software.

Future commercial licenses require the necessary rights. CLA sublicensing never
cancels existing AGPL rights. A wish to prohibit specific competitive uses needs
separate legal/license assessment (`PUBLIC-04` in CONTRIBUTING.md), not terms
inserted into an allegedly unchanged AGPL license.

Before adoption, confirm ownership, registration status (if any), territories,
logo rights, contact and permission/dispute procedures. Publishing this draft
creates no signed brand relationship and asserts no registration.
