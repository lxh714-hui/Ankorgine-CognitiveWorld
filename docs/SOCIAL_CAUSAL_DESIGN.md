## Verified starting point and gaps

- `PlayerDialogueService` records addressed **player-origin** `dialogue_input`; the narrow `NpcDialogueService` path records a selected, owner-bound NPC utterance through the existing `EventProcessor` no-op-world handler. In both paths only the addressed registered recipient receives the trusted `utterance_delivered` Observation and may consume it in its own L1. The Event proves delivery and utterance, not the speaker's claims. NPC-origin speech is an explicitly injected candidate source, appended to the sender's ordinary candidate batch and selected by its `AgentDecision`; it is not an autonomous background conversation loop or general dialogue framework. The selected communication is not a physical Attempt/Resolution. A recipient's existing-Belief review remains independent and governed, and a selected later action still uses Safety, Attempt, Resolution, EventFactory, and EventProcessor.
- `DecisionContext` is an immutable, owner-checked cognition snapshot. Belief, Goal, and SelfModel use canonical owner-scoped Stores. `CognitiveUpdateValidator`/`CognitiveUpdateApplier` govern supported updates with source and stale-value checks. The opt-in cognitive review can propose one revision to an **existing Belief** from newly L1-consumed Observations. It does not establish truth or automatic acceptance of testimony. Current `GoalUpdateProposal` changes an existing Goal's status or priority only; it cannot create a Goal or rewrite its description. SelfModel's tentative evolution is a separate path and is not a persuasion effect.
- The scheduler retains owner-local tension/opportunity findings and also receives a bounded cross-Agent projection derived from registered owners' linked canonical active Goals with typed GoalResourceDemand. Runtime validates the referenced object identity and source. Supported conflict is limited to explicit exclusive-use demands in the same declared scope; potential and shared-target-only findings remain distinct. Each Agent receives only its owner-scoped attention projection. Findings are process-local and cannot create actions, Events, or state changes; general capacity/occupancy proof and durable lifecycle are not implemented.
- `WorldState.relationships` is a canonical mapping of `Relationship(source, target, attributes)`, with initialization and general WorldUpdate storage, but no installed social relationship transition or consent adjudicator. A generic attributes mapping is not a verified relationship or consent schema. Per-slot last-changing Event IDs trace addressable current values only when EventHistory validates the Event and processor receipt; they do not prove the meaning of a relationship or who consented to what.
- Timeline-aware signed EventHistory, SnapshotBundle, Restore Generation fencing, isolated Runtime reconstruction and a narrow in-process RestoreCoordinator publication path are implemented. These do not persist dialogue queues, Observation/L1 receipts, scheduler findings or provider tasks, and do not provide cross-host task isolation or arbitrary host-code sandboxing. Treat social continuity across restore as unsupported unless each required service is explicitly reassembled and revalidated.

## Shared trust and context contract

The minimal social interaction context is an ephemeral, bounded projection for one dialogue delivery or one conflict assessment. It is **not** an Entity, Store, WorldState copy, or authorization token. The future issuer must bind its content to: world/timeline identity and restore generation when available; trusted simulation tick; actor, receiver and other grounded participant references; the exact inbound Event/Observation and L1 receipt where relevant; typed state-slot read receipts for any objective dependency; the owner and version or equivalent exact prior snapshot of each cognition record; and an explicit query/assessment budget. The issuer derives identities and authority from the runtime, not from model text. A model may request more evidence or propose an interpretation; the existing authorization and grounding boundaries decide whether any evidence may be read.

References have different meanings: an Event ID locates a processed occurrence; an Observation ID and L1 receipt establish what a particular owner perceived; a WorldState slot read identifies an objective current value and, where verified, its latest-changing Event. None of these alone proves the semantic truth of a spoken claim, a goal's feasibility, consent, or causation. Unknown, hidden, conflicting, stale, or unsupported data remains marked as such. Runtime-facing internal analysis must never be copied wholesale into an Agent's DecisionContext or player Presentation.

Candidate identity is stable for the same bounded input and semantic issue, independent of Agent registration order, name, model wording, or tick. A candidate carries its origin references, participants, exact assessed state versions, uncertainty, and current lifecycle; it has no Event authority. Provider output is size/budget bounded, structurally validated, and cannot declare itself authorized. No remote model receives owner-private input without the host's existing explicit data-scope approval.

## Social dynamics, information asymmetry and bounded attention

**Approved direction with partial production foundations.** Social Why is the interaction of separately owned objectives, known opportunities and constraints; it is not a shared plot objective or a command to manufacture disagreement. A Goal may sustain repeated attempts, but its existence alone does not establish a physical need, deadline, scarcity, actual obstruction or a complete measure of goal-versus-reality gap. Current `MotivationAssessment` derives configured Goal scores/tensions and capability-blocked cues; `DecisionModulation` adjusts bounded subjective attention/risk/review factors. Those are inputs to the owner's decision, not an automatic Goal transition or world effect.

The installed scheduler finds configured owner-local tension and source-backed opportunities over existing candidates, and separately compares explicit grounded cross-Agent resource demands. A supported demand conflict proves matching exclusive requirements in the same declared scope only. It does not prove an enacted dispute, private intention known by another NPC, physical scarcity or a future fight. Cooperation and competition can be equally plausible candidate interpretations; general cooperation discovery, obligations, resource capacity and deadline/pressure dynamics have not been implemented. Personality, evidence, capabilities and each owner's legal decision options continue to differ. An opportunity need not be chosen or succeed; refusal and conflict remain legitimate outcomes under independent Safety, WorldNorms and Resolution responsibilities.

Information asymmetry is preserved through the existing L1/Observation and Character-linked Memory boundaries. NPC A may act on one subjective belief and NPC B on another without either belief becoming world truth. A system task can compare only its separately approved minimal fields; its broader view cannot be copied into an NPC prompt, become evidence of a heard claim, or reveal another owner's private Goal. Task-driven retrieval must identify which principal needs which condition, verify authorized source/time/Timeline and keep insufficient or contradictory evidence explicit. The new semantic requirements in DATA_CONTRACTS.md do not implement a unified social-context service or permit owner-wide cognition scans.

For future traceable social feedback, retain separate links for **statement → verified delivery → recipient Observation/L1 consumption → recipient interpretation → governed Belief decision → later selected action → actual Event/outcome → subsequent supporting or contradicting evidence**. Each link has a different issuer and proof scope: the utterance Event proves neither its claim nor the receiver's acceptance, while a later objective Event cannot retroactively prove what the receiver knew. A recipient may retain a false or uncertain Belief despite additional computation. The implemented slices above cover only selected links; there is no general attribution graph, durable social-experience learner or automatic contradiction-resolution policy. Any resource allocation for this review remains separate from social truth, owner access and world-event authority; see [ARCHITECTURE.md](ARCHITECTURE.md#world-dynamics-task-information-and-bounded-exploration).

A future closed dynamics slice may follow typed goal/condition gap → potential conflict or cooperation opportunity → owner context and independent candidate selection → ordinary Resolution/EventProcessor → changed state → newly authorized evidence and governed later reassessment. Current Core scheduled turns and narrow finding/score integration cover parts of this path, while a desktop autonomous driver, general condition-gap vocabulary, Event-to-Goal reactivation and durable finding lifecycle are absent. World consequences never directly rewrite a Goal or Belief: applicable cognitive updates still require their existing evidence/owner/prior-state governance. Current Goal updates cover existing status/priority only; the locked-door BehaviorFeedback slice is not a general social consequence learner. No `AttributedSocialExperience/v1` record, temporal testimony comparator or typed experience-driven scoring is installed by this design.

New actors, organizations or relationships may be proposed as hypothetical content, not inferred as already existing from a model sentence. Existing content validation/registry/installation may support its declared Character/Location/Scenario/entity scope; it is not a general organization/relationship transition authority. Instantiating a new domain or changing an objective relationship needs a reviewed typed contract and the applicable content/Resolution/EventProcessor path. Unsupported domains remain unresolved. A candidate future or scheduler finding cannot add a person, obligation, trust change or relation to WorldState, Memory or player Presentation.

Social Why findings may inform Where allocation under future `POST-MVP-02`/`POST-MVP-06` work: importance, consequence, uncertainty, cost, diversity and expiry are candidate policy cues, not fixed narrative weights. Current per-owner ranking/dormancy and candidate-tree budgets are partial foundations; no unified Token/latency/LOD allocator exists. More compute does not expand knowledge rights, and less compute does not establish that cooperation or an unfamiliar solution is impossible. Deferred/pruned/provider-failed paths retain those meanings, rather than becoming causal rejections. Future work must distinguish unknown conditions from verified prohibitions and may request bounded evidence or alternatives while preserving the original Runtime/Timeline and commit boundaries.

The social implementation and restoration limits below remain in force. Current signed D1/D2 proof and D3 MemoryDecision foundations do not persist general social dynamics, dialogue queues or tree/provider tasks. Any later lifecycle persistence must use the existing canonical Memory/snapshot and verified ancestor contracts, not a new social authority Store. The roadmap's existing social deferrals and D3.2.4 open ancestor debt are not closed by this direction.

## Reference-policy deviation (candidate mathematics; not a production classifier)

“Non-rational” here means deviation relative to an explicitly justified reference policy, not a psychological diagnosis or an objectively false action. This is an uncalibrated modeling proposal, separate from current Emotion/Motivation modulation, candidate scores and the narrow BehaviorFeedback mechanism. Existing scores are not automatically calibrated utility. No Belief, Goal, SelfModel or world revision is authorized.

The reference must use the NPC's applicable Beliefs and acquired information, stable Goals/preferences, risk attitude, time horizon, known constraints, exploration value, strategic deception and commitment costs. Incomplete information, legitimate preference change, deliberate sacrifice, value trade-offs and ordinary stochastic choice are not automatically non-rational. Missing reference support must allow “insufficient to classify”; omniscient world truth must not replace the owner's perspective.

### Alternative A: normalized opportunity loss

For a fixed, explicit finite candidate set \(A\), with comparable reference utility \(U(a)\), let \(U_{\max}=\max_A U\) and \(U_{\min}=\min_A U\). If \(U_{\max}>U_{\min}\),

\[
D_R(a)=\frac{U_{\max}-U(a)}{U_{\max}-U_{\min}}\in[0,1].
\]

Positive affine utility transformations preserve this score. It can assess one selected action, but depends on the candidate set and extreme options. LOD pruning may change the normalization range without changing behavior. If all utilities tie, report that the reference cannot distinguish; do not insert arbitrary epsilon to force classification. Low reference utility alone is not evidence of irrationality.

### Alternative B: policy-distribution deviation

With adequate samples from comparable contexts or explicit justified distributions over the same support,

\[
D_{\mathrm{TV}}=\frac12\sum_{a\in A}|\pi(a)-\pi_0(a)|\in[0,1].
\]

\(\pi\) and \(\pi_0\) are normalized actual/reference policies. This allows stochastic or exploratory references, but a single low-probability action does not establish distributional abnormality. Multiple defensible policies can differ; this score alone does not diagnose irrationality. Sampling support and context comparability require review and do not advance the blocked sample-count workstream.

If a reference uses \(\pi_0(a)\propto\exp(U(a)/T)\), require \(T>0\) with a scale compatible with utility. Under \(U'=cU+d\), \(c>0\), scale \(T'=cT\) to preserve the distribution. No production softmax policy is approved here.

### Candidate threshold, intensity and emotional input

For a supported \(D\in[0,1]\), a candidate flag is \(D>\tau\), with intensity

\[
J=\left[\frac{\max(0,D-\tau)}{1-\tau}\right]^p,
\qquad 0\le\tau<1,\quad p>0.
\]

The score is continuous at the threshold and nondecreasing above it; \(p>1\) can give a smooth first derivative there. \(\tau=1\) needs separate endpoint semantics and cannot be substituted into this formula. Reject out-of-range, NaN, infinite or otherwise invalid parameters. Threshold/exponent configuration needs explicit versioning, calibration and intended use; no production classifier or configuration schema is approved.

A separate candidate behavior-generation hypothesis is

\[
\pi(a)\propto\exp\left(\frac{\widehat U(a)+\lambda H(a,e)}{T}\right).
\]

\(\widehat U\) is applicable scaled reference utility, \(e\) the owner's emotional state, \(H\) an explicitly defined emotional/impulsive action drive, \(\lambda\) its relative scale and \(T>0\) a compatible temperature. Their domains/scales and validation remain undecided. High emotion may cause little deviation; large deviation need not imply high emotion. If emotion reflects a legitimate preference change, revise the reference interpretation rather than pre-labeling the choice irrational.

All candidates still pass existing authorization, supported mechanism checks, Resolution and EventProcessor. Intensity cannot determine physical effects or Belief truth. The model adds no cognitive revision authority; existing [three-axis boundaries](ARCHITECTURE.md#visual-and-auditory-lod-mathematics-candidate-models-uncalibrated) and B1/B2/B3 proof limits remain intact.

Future deterministic/model-validation cases must include utility rescaling, equal and conflicting goals, candidate-set pruning, deliberate long-term sacrifice, incomplete owner Beliefs, high emotion/small deviation, low emotion/large deviation, threshold endpoints and invalid numbers. Historical score calibration cannot be silently reinterpreted after a model upgrade. Reference policy, data, parameters, use as a diagnostic versus a probability, and calibration acceptance remain maintainer decisions; no test result is claimed.

## Route A: Persuasion — recipient-owned cognition

**Implemented in-process slice.** `PlayerDialogueService` can receive an explicitly injected cognitive review Provider after player speech, and `NpcDialogueService` can deliver the narrow NPC-origin utterance described below. After either trusted `dialogue_input` is delivered, only the addressed NPC's newly consumed L1 Observation initiates its own review. The bounded request contains that speech fact, this owner's existing Beliefs and separately consumed, processor-backed objective Observations. Speech proves utterance, never the truth of its contents. A revision must cite the speech and at least one independent recipient Observation. The service rechecks source/Event provenance, L1 receipt, simulation tick and the prior Belief snapshot before reusing `CognitiveBeliefReview → CognitiveUpdateValidator → CognitiveUpdateApplier → BeliefStore`. Provider absence, failure, no revision, insufficient support, stale state and replay leave the Belief unchanged. Later AgentTurn reads the canonical record. This is an opt-in subjective review: a cited independent fact is not a general semantic entailment proof, and no real Ollama persuasion-quality acceptance is claimed. There is no default external model call, sender-controlled acceptance, Goal/Personality update, objective relationship effect, or persistence/restore fencing.

### NPC-origin communication v0.1 — narrow in-process slice

`SimulationRuntime` can enable this path only with an explicitly injected `NpcCommunicationCandidateProvider`. For the current sender turn, the provider receives a bounded owner-scoped assessment and may propose at most one utterance to a currently registered co-located recipient, citing only sender-authorized references. The proposal joins the sender's existing candidate batch; the regular `AgentDecision` scores and selects it. Only the selected proposal is rechecked and recorded as `dialogue_input` by `NpcDialogueService` through `EventProcessor`. It creates a trusted recipient-only Observation but no separate inbox, Attempt, Resolution, or WorldState change. The recipient must consume that Observation in its own L1 before it can enter the existing cognitive review; the review distinguishes testimony from independent objective evidence and can accept, reject, or defer a revision to an existing Belief through the existing validator/applier/store chain. A later recipient AgentTurn reads the canonical Belief.

The deterministic integration test demonstrates a candidate/score/selection and a downstream Belief-to-choice difference using injected test policies and a fake reviewer. It does not establish default-provider behavior, general language truth, semantic entailment, real-model quality, durable delivery/idempotency, restore continuity for transient Observations, NPC-NPC automatic replies, Goal persuasion, or objective relationship/consent effects. Unsupported, malformed, stale, non-co-located, unselected, or unauthorised proposals fail closed or remain ordinary candidate-free turns.

1. Agent A may submit a bounded communication candidate through the explicit provider seam. The ordinary AgentDecision must select it; delivery then authenticates A as the speaker and passes the existing trusted Event/Observation boundary. An addressed Observation reaches B's L1. Until L1 consumption is verified, the speech cannot be used as B's cognitive evidence. Sender identity, actual actor, and any original initiator stay distinct.
2. The current Belief slice assembles the receiver's newly consumed speech Observation, bounded existing Belief snapshots, and independently authorized recipient Observations. Goal review remains future design. It distinguishes **speech delivered**, **a claim supported by independent evidence**, and **the receiver's subjective acceptance or rejection**. The sender's assertion, a model's assessment, and a source ID cannot independently establish an objective fact.
3. An explicitly configured semantic provider may propose a bounded interpretation with uncertainty and cited Observation IDs, or no revision. A proposed existing-Belief revision still needs independent recipient evidence and governed application; outward replies and voluntary actions remain with `AgentDecision`. Clarification or refusal does not itself authorize a Belief write. General social acceptance and Goal persuasion remain unimplemented.
4. An accepted Belief proposal cites the receiver's actual evidence, exact owner and prior record snapshot. The dialogue service checks Observation/Event pairing, L1 receipt and current tick, then reuses `BeliefUpdateProposal → CognitiveUpdateValidator → CognitiveUpdateApplier → BeliefStore`. The generic validator alone does not enforce all persuasion-specific proof. For an existing Goal, only status/priority changes currently fit `GoalUpdateProposal`; new goals or changed descriptions need a separate justified canonical contract. No speech may directly alter Personality or SelfModel.
5. No candidate, refusal, clarification, provider failure, or insufficient evidence automatically changes cognition. Duplicate delivery/review is tied to an unchanged inbound Event and receiver L1 receipt, with a consumed-update marker or equivalent idempotency check at application. A stale prior record, altered evidence, changed owner, or competing update fails and may be reconsidered from fresh context. A partial provider batch must not leave a partially applied update. Later AgentTurn reads the canonical result only after successful governed application.

The outcome is a **subjective update of B**, never a statement that A was objectively right. B may disbelieve true speech, believe false speech, or remain uncertain. Any subsequent action is a new Attempt that passes Safety and Resolution. Speech, testimony, belief revision, and objective relationship change have separate provenance and outcomes.

## Route B: Cross-Agent Goal Conflict — bounded system attention

1. **Implemented / narrow:** `SimulationRuntime` performs a host-level comparison by visiting registered owners in stable ID order, obtaining each registered Agent's own `CognitionContext`, and reading only its Character-linked active canonical Goals. A Goal may contain typed `GoalResourceDemand(resource_ref, scope_ref, exclusive_use)` records. Free text, Goal source citations, private Belief/Memory/Emotion/SelfModel and full `DecisionContext` are not used to infer or describe another owner's goal.
2. Each demand is projected only after its exact `object:{id}` resolves to an existing WorldState Entity and the `entities/{id}/type` slot read is current, source-verifiable, and bound to this simulation tick. The opaque projection carries owner, Goal ID/revision, typed demand, tick, and slot receipt; it contains no Goal description or priority. Missing/stale/invalid resource state is omitted. This is a trusted system comparison, not information granted to either Agent.
3. The current statuses are distinct: `supported_conflict` requires two different owners' active canonical Goals to demand exclusive use of the same grounded object in the same explicit scope; `potential_conflict` means exclusive-use intent is present but its shared scope is not established; `shared_target_only` means a shared grounded target without an exclusive-use constraint. These describe Goal requirements only, not actual resource capacity, possession, hostility, awareness, action, or enacted world conflict. No resource-capacity model currently exists.
4. The existing `ConflictOpportunityScheduler` issues an owner-specific finding. Its Goal list and evidence refs include only that owner's Goal and a digest of the grounded slot receipt; a partner Goal ID, prose, priority, and private reasoning are omitted. A per-owner opaque finding identity includes the participant Goal revisions internally for deduplication without making those identities visible in the finding. Same-state repeats update the existing process-local lifecycle; changed Goal revision/resource receipt changes the identity, while absence can make prior findings dormant after the existing bounded missed-turn policy.
5. Findings are delivered only through the current owner's existing AgentDecision evaluation path; they may contribute the existing bounded `scheduler_relevance` only to candidates linked to that owner's Goal. Finding IDs may appear in that owner's Proposal trace and Event Tree diagnostic branches. Shared-target-only relevance is zero. Each Agent still decides independently. Findings create no Attempt/Event/WorldUpdate and modify no Goal/cognition. Lifecycle is in-process, not durable; restore fencing and cross-process persistence remain future design.

## Relationship, consent, and authority

The current `Relationship` record has stable endpoints and open attributes, but those attributes do not yet define typed social claims, allowed transitions, visibility, temporal scope, or consent semantics. A spoken promise, refusal, or apparent agreement is initially an utterance occurrence and, if delivered, an observer-specific fact of hearing it. Whether it represents valid consent, a durable obligation, or a relationship change remains unresolved without a separately installed contract that specifies participants, scope, timing, revocation/expiry, capacity, evidence, and authorization. Do not add one Boolean `consent` field or treat the speaker's words as universal permission.

An Agent's attitude, Belief, or Goal belongs to that Agent's canonical cognition Store and changes only through its governance. An objective relationship state belongs to canonical WorldState and needs a typed, validated WorldUpdate through `Resolution → EventFactory → EventProcessor`. No such general social relationship policy is installed. WorldNorms may evaluate conduct but do not decide persuasion success, compel cooperation, or create punishment. P0 Safety still applies independently and must not suppress ordinary fictional disagreement or competition.

## Timeline and restore alignment (future contract)

Every future issued social context, provider request, candidate, validation receipt, and pending application needs the trusted `(timeline_id, restore_generation, simulation_tick)` or equivalent host-issued identity, plus owner/participants, exact evidence refs, prior cognition version, and a stable consumption key. Merely adding fields does not provide isolation. The trusted runtime must reject an application if its generation or timeline differs from the active one, its Event/Observation is absent from that branch, its L1 receipt belongs to an abandoned runtime, or its prior state no longer matches. On restore, Context Freeze stops new turns and commits; it invalidates outstanding model calls, scheduler findings, Event Tree snapshots, dialogue working context, and transient WorkingMemory. Cognition Restore reloads each owner's persistent Belief/Goal/SelfModel/Memory state from the selected checkpoint and rebuilds ephemeral caches. Shared ancestors remain immutable, while branch-local cognition, EventHistory, WorldState, and slot provenance diverge. Core save/timeline and in-process restore-generation infrastructure now exists, but these broader social-state guarantees remain future requirements: dialogue queues, transient L1 receipts and scheduler/provider work are not durable components, and cross-process stale-task fencing is not provided.

An in-process slice may be accepted before persistence using current tick, exact Event/Observation/L1 bindings, prior snapshots, and process-local deduplication. Its tests must explicitly state that old-generation rejection after a real restore is still pending. A future persistence slice must inject delayed provider results during restore and verify no abandoned-future cognition or world effects survive.

## Minimum acceptance slices and tests

**Persuasion first slice:** one trusted inbound utterance to B, verified B-only L1 consumption, independent evidence where available, a receiver-owned bounded interpretation, rejection/uncertainty and one supported existing-Belief revision option, governed validation/application, and a later B turn reading the updated canonical Belief. Tests must cover false testimony, true but rejected speech, supported acceptance, clarification, no evidence, provider failure, altered/other-owner Observation, duplicate input, stale prior Belief, and no WorldState/EventHistory or A-cognition write from the review. An existing Goal status/priority revision may follow once its receiver acceptance and source validation are demonstrated; new Goal content requires a separate contract.

**Cross-Agent first slice (implemented, bounded):** two active canonical Goals with structured resource demands over a grounded Entity, distinct Goal status classes, owner-specific projections, deduplication, process-local dormant lifecycle, candidate-trace/Event Tree linkage, and no world/cognition side effect. Tests vary scope, exclusivity, missing resource, registration order, repeat discovery, and Goal completion. Current supported-conflict evidence is limited to explicit exclusive-use claims sharing the same declared scope; it is not a typed WorldState capacity model. Remaining work: typed resource capacity/availability semantics and Event-driven changes, broader resource/relationship evidence, durable lifecycle, restore generation, and independent semantic review of Goal claims.

**Restore acceptance, later:** checkpoint/branch tests for recipient-only cognition, internal issue lifecycle, stale provider response rejection, save replacement failure, and no abandoned-future observation, belief, goal, or relationship source after restore. These tests cannot be claimed by current in-process fixtures.

## Open decisions and prohibited shortcuts

Open design questions: how to represent grounded resource demand/capacity without encoding every conflict type; which host capability may perform cross-owner comparison; what evidence supports subjective trust in testimony without treating it as objective truth; how a recipient governs Goal content creation if needed; whether an objective relationship transition is justified and what its typed scope is; and how save recovery persists issue lifecycle without creating a second cognition or world authority.

Do not equate utterance with consent or truth, matching Goal targets with conflict, conflict detection with Agent knowledge, or model confidence with authorization. Do not read all private Stores into a model prompt, clone WorldState for social analysis, let the sender modify B's cognition, let the scheduler create an Event, or bypass Safety, Resolution, EventFactory, EventProcessor, canonical cognition Stores, Context Freeze, or restore-generation fencing. Unsupported effects stay unresolved.

**L1 consumption proof v0.1:** A trusted directed utterance may leave a signed, owner-scoped system record after the receiver's actual L1 consumption. The issuer binds the exact Observation to source Event processing evidence and historical visibility; isolated restore repeats those checks. The record is persisted through the existing canonical cognition snapshot but is not an NPC semantic MemoryDecision. It proves a narrow consumption event, not truth of speech, recipient acceptance, social experience, relationship change or later behavior. Socialization v0.2 needs its own governed interpretation and effect tests.

### Failure and lifecycle states

Keep these states distinct: (1) Observation issued from trusted Event; (2) visible/eligible to owner at event time; (3) actually consumed by owner's L1; (4) proof signed; (5) proof committed to canonical MemoryStore; (6) included in a published signed Save Bundle; (7) reverified during isolated restore. L1 consumption alone is process-local. A crash before consumption creates no proof; after consumption but before signing leaves no durable proof and restore must not backfill; after signing but before canonical commit leaves no canonical proof; after commit but before a published save, the last checkpoint need not contain it. Interrupted publication follows SaveManager transaction recovery. Replay of a committed stable key returns the same proof; a different digest under that key conflicts. Restore verifies source Event, ancestry, historical rule, owner, Observation digest and signature before exposing it.

### Stage 0 implementation dependencies

- Freeze the typed source/Event extension and compatibility rules first. D1 claim binding and D2 non-verbal proof can then be separate vertical slices; integration tests must confirm the same immutable Event/evidence semantics.
- D3 is required before D5 persistence. D4 is required for next-turn access. D3/D4 may be developed in parallel only after association ownership and authorization semantics are jointly frozen; integration is serial.
- D5 identity/revisions depend on D1–D3. D6 depends on claim scope, domain mapping and complete-history coverage. Until D6 exists, comparison returns insufficient/incomparable.
- D7 depends on D4–D6 and existing Belief governance. Scoring work follows a typed Belief-to-candidate relation.
- D8 is cross-cutting: snapshot codec/restore tests accompany each persistent type. Bundle version is decided by signed manifest semantics, not field count.
- D9 is the final integration gate after the preceding contracts; it includes independent-process save/restore, leases, branch isolation and continued Runtime execution.

## NPC Socialization v0.2 — Stage 2.0 D3/D4 freeze (design only)

This contract was frozen before implementation. D3 now has a separate formal path: `MemoryDecisionEngine` requires explicit host dependencies and policy installation, while the old `MemoryDecisionAuthority.issue(accepted=...)` remains a Memory/v1 compatibility path only. Formal v2 writes require the policy-bound decision, Factory request, final MemoryPersistence fence and Store guard. D4 remains unimplemented: retrieval uses captured explicit `memory_ids` and has no dynamic grant index or current-lineage authorization review.

### D3 — formal MemoryDecision/v1

- The host explicitly installs a versioned `policy_id`/`policy_version` during Runtime assembly. No policy, unknown policy version, exception or malformed result means no accepted decision and no write. This is a replaceable host policy, not autonomous NPC memory consolidation.
- Callers and models may submit only a `MemoryProposal`. `MemoryDecisionInput/v1` is an immutable canonical projection binding owner and trusted Agent/Character identity, proposal type/schema/digest, verified typed source references, constrained requested access scope, active policy reference and only necessary current revision data. It excludes all-owner Memory scans and unrelated cognition by default. External Providers remain opt-in and require host data-disclosure authorization.
- Only the installed policy invocation can produce typed `accepted`, `rejected` or `deferred` output for the exact input digest. `MemoryDecisionAuthority` seals that invocation-bound result; it must not accept a caller's bare `accepted=True`. The sealed `MemoryDecision/v1` binds outcome, proposal digest/identity, owner, source set, policy/version, bounded reason code and original session/Timeline/restore-generation permit. The Python in-process object boundary is not protection against hostile same-process code.
- Rejected/deferred decisions have no MemoryStore effect and are not persisted in a new audit Store. Deferred is not an automatic retry; later review is an explicit fresh policy invocation. Accepted is necessary, never sufficient, for writing. Factory and Persistence revalidate all bindings under the existing final fence.
- Identical accepted retries are idempotent. Same record identity with changed content/evidence/decision/grants conflicts. Any revision uses an explicit new immutable identity and predecessor reference; it must not rely on today's `MemoryStore` overwrite. Accepted provenance is a typed field on the canonical Memory record, not metadata; rejected/deferred audit durability is outside this slice.

### D4 — dynamic owner/Character authorization

- A newly authorized semantic Memory carries typed `MemoryCharacterGrant/v1` data inside the canonical record, bound to its owner and Character. The host policy explicitly approves the grant; final Persistence validates the owner/Character pair against the current trusted Runtime roster. The current roster maps one Agent to one Character, so v1 permits only the registered Character of the same owner; cross-owner sharing and multiple Characters per Agent are unsupported and fail closed.
- Each AgentTurn obtains a trusted retrieval principal from the active Runtime roster and current Character context. Caller-supplied query owner/IDs are not authority. Candidates are the union of validated new grants and existing explicit legacy `Character.memory_ids`; there is no owner-wide scan. A derived `(owner, character)` index only locates candidates. Retrieval re-reads each canonical record and validates schema, semantic eligibility, owner/grant, source Event in the verified active lineage and current revision eligibility.
- L1 system proofs remain system-only even if a legacy Character list names their IDs. Unknown record types/schemas deny retrieval by default. Ordinary retrieval cannot turn proof records into social experience or authorize other cognition/WorldState writes.
- The index is derived and noncanonical. Store and index update/invalidation share the existing write-fence commit boundary; a stale/unavailable index is rebuilt from verified canonical records under a consistent read boundary or retrieval fails closed. Restore verifies grants against the independent Agent/Character manifest and EventHistory ancestry, then rebuilds the index in the isolated Store before activation. No index entry is persisted as authority.
- Legacy Memory and snapshot v2 retain their original meaning. Explicit old `memory_ids` remain explicit legacy links only; they are not upgraded to new grants. Child Timelines inherit only verified ancestor records and their unchanged grants; parent/child suffixes remain disjoint. Source eligibility uses verified EventHistory lineage, never current WorldState to reinterpret past visibility.

### Joint transaction and version decision

Write: installed policy → authority-sealed decision → factory-issued request → one final `RuntimeWriteFence.commit_scope()` that checks original permit, proposal/decision digest, owner, typed sources, active EventHistory, trusted Character grant and immutable identity → canonical Memory commit plus atomic derived-index update/invalidation. A failed commit cannot leave an index-only result. Read and snapshot capture share compatible consistency boundaries, so readers/capture observe the pre-commit state or the complete committed record/grant, never a mixed state. Restore verifies records and lineage before rebuilding the derived index in isolation; only RestoreCoordinator publication exposes it.

**Stage 0 schema decision, updated by Stage 2.1 Phase 1:** Memory/v1 retains its original seven-field canonical encoding and interpretation. Memory/v2 now carries typed `MemoryDecisionProvenance/v1`, a logical record identity, an immutable record/revision identity (`Memory.id`), and an optional predecessor record reference. CognitiveStateSnapshot v2 remains readable and is verified with its original purpose; new captures use schema/purpose v3 and sign the complete Memory/v2 fields. SnapshotBundle v2 remains compatible because its signed component membership and manifest semantics are unchanged and the exact v3 cognition bytes are digest-bound; its verifier performs internal schema validation. Old v2 data receives no inferred provenance or grant. This implementation phase does not change SaveManager, decision, write authorization, or Runtime behavior.

**Stage 2.1 Phase 2 — D3 formal MemoryDecision (implemented):** A host explicitly constructs the decision engine with its policy, authority, timeline-bound EventHistory and RuntimeWriteFence. Canonical bounded JSON proposal/input digests, policy identity/version and a unique call ID are bound to the accepted result. The new `MemoryDecisionProvenance/v2` records the accepted outcome and invocation context without changing the prior provenance/v1 or old snapshot bytes. The legacy caller-supplied `Authority.issue(accepted=...)` path can still write Memory/v1 but cannot produce Memory/v2. Persistence and Store revalidate the formal request under the original fence; direct v2 Store commits without the Persistence capability are refused. Rejected/deferred results have no Store effect. This is an in-process supported-path boundary, not protection from arbitrary hostile same-process code.

**Stage 2.1 Phase 3 — D3.2 branch-local revision ancestry (implemented):** A current branch's visible Memory/v2 revisions are the exact records inherited from its recursively verified signed checkpoint plus revisions authored on that Timeline. Snapshot validation, isolated reconstruction and fenced Store commits reject parent post-fork or sibling-only records, changed/omitted inherited records, missing predecessors, cycles, multiple roots, duplicate successors and ambiguous tips. Separate child branches may independently revise the same inherited tip. This does not implement D4 grants or D5 social-experience effects.

**Stage 2.0.1 boundary closure:** canonical L1 proof payloads carry the typed `SystemOnlyMemoryContent` marker. The MemoryStore semantic-retrievability contract is enforced by `CognitionContext`, `MemoryRetriever`, and `MemorySnapshot.from_memory`; a Character's explicit Memory ID cannot expose either verbal or nonverbal proofs. Known system discriminators with inconsistent payloads and unknown payloads carrying the system-only marker fail closed. Snapshot persistence and trusted proof verification retain internal canonical Store access. This closes the retrieval prerequisite only; it does not implement D3/D4. The current one-Agent/one-Character registry mapping still limits D4 v1 as stated above.

### Revision and revocation resolution

Authorization belongs to one immutable Memory revision: stable logical record ID, unique revision ID, and optional `supersedes_record_id`. A successor is a new canonical Memory ID, never an overwrite. Retrieval uses only the verified chain tip on the active Timeline; an accepted revocation revision has no active Character grant. Cycles, missing predecessors, conflicting tips on one Timeline, mismatched owners/sources, or an indeterminate tip deny that logical record. Parent and child resolve tips independently, so a child revision cannot alter its parent's authorization. This is the minimum lifecycle needed for D4, not implementation of D5 comparison.


## Social physical room slice

**Current opt-in implementation, narrow D4/D5 subset:** `SocialExperienceService` reviews one selected NPC's typed claim only after the recipient holds both exact signed L1 proof records. It reuses MemoryDecision/MemoryPersistence/MemoryStore, owner/Character association and signed snapshots. This supersedes the earlier baseline's blanket absence statement only for this explicitly supported slice; it is not full Socialization v0.2 or B4.

The comparator checks exact Event-scoped typed equality, not narrative persuasion strength. Merely speaking is not recipient observation; recipient observation is not consumption; consumption is not understanding, agreement or truth. Unsupported scope/types/proofs produce no experience. A retained experience says only that one attributed claim agrees/disagrees with the recipient's independently consumed fact. It is not a universal trust score or objective relationship change.

The explicit host policy can retain that assessment, propose an existing-Belief revision with its evidence, and propose a new Goal. Generic source/owner governance and stale-state checks remain mandatory. The room's configurable willingness-to-act policy demonstrates different subsequent decisions for the same verified experience; contradicted testimony and withholding acceptance need not produce the same Goal. Goal creation is separate from existing status/priority updates; its description is not silently rewritten.

Run the source checkout with `py -3.14 run_social_room.py --save-root <new-directory>`. Each invocation creates an independent Timeline; reusing the save directory opens its existing trust root but does not automatically load an earlier checkpoint. The executable host supplies a room, two registered NPCs, a key tool, static table/drawer hierarchy and a keyed locked door. The first round selects speech and recipient evidence review; the next may select an actual key attempt. Installed Door Resolution uses authoritative tool ownership/door state and EventProcessor commits effects. In the missing-key variant the key remains in the drawer and the method text cannot supply ownership. Pickup and drawer opening are explicitly unsupported.

A signed SaveManager checkpoint preserves the physical state, Belief/Goal values, consumption proofs, experience payload and Character links. The strict SocialRoomAssembler supplies content/policies and verified roster links; it does not regenerate accepted history. Child review reuses ancestor experience. Fresh authorized door observation supplies the resumed decision context, and a second-turn door change remains branch-local. This is a programmatic/CLI world; desktop dialogue integration and real-model semantic quality are not verified by this slice. Exact fields and compatibility are in [DATA_CONTRACTS](DATA_CONTRACTS.md#social-experience-v1-and-goal-creation).


## Owner specific social response slice

The source-checkout command `py -3.14 run_social_room.py --contrasting-receivers --save-root <directory>` runs an optional sender/two-recipient scene. The proposition and utterance text match, but each delivery remains its own recipient-bound formal Event and Observation; this is not a broadcast authorization shortcut.

Both sighted recipients can retain the same supported SocialExperience while their saved Personality preferences lead one to create a Goal and try the keyed door and the other to wait. Their factual Beliefs can converge on the independently observed value without requiring identical action commitments. Counterfactual configuration verifies that the additional recipient can also acquire the Goal when its own preference is true; names/roles do not prescribe the outcome. A recipient lacking independent visual evidence can consume the addressed speech yet retain its prior Belief, have an insufficient assessment, and create no Goal. Contradicted evidence and a deferred Memory policy are separate cases; mere reception is never treated as agreement.

Root `accept_testimony` remains an initialization convenience for the original recipient. RoomSocialPolicy/v2 copies owner preferences from canonical Personality, including on restore, instead of consulting that host switch during cognition. Host configuration changes cannot reinterpret a retained observed value or overwrite saved preferences. This is a content-specific willingness policy, not a calibrated general trust or non-rational behavior model.

Each speech exchange offers one autonomous sender choice, lets it finish, then schedules the recipient on a later tick. This fixes the real recipient-ID ordering gap without modifying AgentScheduler, repeating a committed utterance or manufacturing consumption. The subsequent receiver-action round remains a normal AgentDecision → Attempt → Resolution → EventFactory → EventProcessor path.

Signed checkpoint tests cover cold reconstruction of the saved parent, child and sibling forks, distinct physical suffixes, unchanged ancestor proof records and continuation from fresh authorized door observations. The assembler explicitly reinjects executable content/policies; it neither restores an old task permit nor replays historical speech. The diagnostic comparison/Goal summary is for developer acceptance, not a player-facing private-cognition projection. See [transient policy/diagnostic semantics](DATA_CONTRACTS.md#owner-specific-room-policy).


## Goal completion in the room

The room's new evidence-derived opening Goal explicitly declares an object target and the typed `environment_state / door_open = true` fact for that object. This door-specific declaration belongs to injected content; the generic adapter only compares an explicit fact condition. A selected action is bound to exactly one Goal through the existing signed B3 Attempt. Another actor-action Goal, even with the same fact condition, cannot claim that action's result. Explicit GoalCompletionCondition/v2 state commitments instead use the owner's own authorized, actually consumed environment-state evidence, including a state produced by another actor; this never transfers the producer's B3 provenance or completes an observer's actor-action Goal.

The runnable CLI schedules a bounded subsequent recipient round after the physical-action round. Actual owner L1 consumption of the committed result enables an existing governed status update to completed, and the following decision waits instead of reopening the door for that Goal. The CLI reports completed Goals before publishing its checkpoint; it is developer acceptance output, not private cognition sent to player chat. A successful action with an unmet condition, a formal failure or an uncommitted result does not complete the Goal. Delivery is not consumption and success is not necessarily objective fulfillment.

Focused tests cover owner-bound completion, unrelated Goals, duplicate handling, governance refusal, uncertain-write capture freeze, legacy no-condition behavior, signed save/restore and separate parent/child/sibling states. The current qualified-address missing-key path has a separate existing B1 validation limitation: `object:ID` is compared directly to raw environment slot IDs and rejects before formal commitment. A supported bare-object-address fixture verifies a genuinely committed failure remains active; the qualified-address regression explicitly verifies noncommit and no completion. This limitation is recorded in [the debt table](DEVELOPMENT_ROADMAP.md#owner-specific-social-response-status), not hidden as a successful failure Event.

General planning, multi-action fulfillment, completion from arbitrary current world conditions, automatic recovery of unconsumed results and historical completion-policy attestation remain outside this slice. [Condition and persistence details](DATA_CONTRACTS.md#consumed-result-goal-completion).
